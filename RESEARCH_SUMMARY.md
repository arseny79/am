## Critical Missing Features ❌

### The Problem:

MSP M&A transactions are **complex, 90-day processes** with many steps:
1. Prepare sales packet (financial data, client lists, tech stack)
2. Qualify buyers (filter "tire kickers" from serious acquirers)
3. Meet buyers and receive offers
4. Compare multiple offers
5. Sign LOI (Letter of Intent)
6. Complete due diligence (50-100 document requests)
7. Negotiate final agreement
8. Close and transition

**Your platform supports steps 3-4 only.** The rest have no tooling.

### What Traditional Brokers Provide (That We Don't):

1. **Sales Packet Templates** - Spreadsheets, checklists, guidance on what to prepare
2. **Buyer Qualification** - Verify proof of funds, filter unqualified buyers
3. **LOI Templates** - Legal documents for offer acceptance
4. **Due Diligence Checklists** - Structured 50-100 item lists with tracking
5. **Multiple Offer Comparison** - Side-by-side analysis tools
6. **Flexible Deal Structures** - Partial sales, mergers, earn-outs (not just 100% cash sales)

**Result:** Sellers choose brokers because they provide **process guidance**, not just a listing board.
