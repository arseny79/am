# mspsmarket.com Pricing Model

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Overview

mspsmarket.com uses a hybrid revenue model combining success fees with optional premium features. This approach aligns platform incentives with user success while providing immediate revenue streams.

---

## Primary Revenue: Success Fees

Success fees are charged on completed transactions facilitated through the platform.

### Fee Structure

| Deal Size | Success Fee | Example Deal | Platform Revenue |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|------------------|
| Up to $5M | **3%** | $2M MSP sale | $60,000 |
| $5M - $20M | **2%** | $10M MSP sale | $200,000 |
| Above $20M | **1%** | $50M MSP sale | $500,000 |

### How It Works

The success fee structure is tiered and applies to the entire transaction amount. Buyers and sellers negotiate who pays the fee, or they can split it.

**Example Calculation:**
- **Deal Value:** $8M
- **Fee Tier:** $5M - $20M
- **Success Fee:** 2% × $8M = **$160,000**

### Payment Terms

- Success fee is due upon deal closing
- Payment processed through Escrow.com integration
- Funds released after transaction completion verification
- Net 30 payment terms available for enterprise buyers

### What Qualifies as a "Completed Transaction"

A transaction is considered complete when:
1. Purchase agreement is signed by both parties
2. Funds have been transferred (or escrow released)
3. Asset transfer is complete
4. Both parties confirm completion on the platform

## Secondary Revenue: Premium Listings

Optional paid listing upgrades for sellers who want enhanced visibility.

### Listing Tiers

#### Free Basic Listing
**$0/month**

- Standard search visibility
- All core features (NDA protection, deal room, messaging)
- Unlimited duration
- Basic analytics (views, NDA signatures)

**Best for:** Sellers testing the platform or with niche businesses

#### Featured Listing
**$499/month**

Everything in Basic, plus:
- ✨ **Homepage featured section placement**
- 🔝 **Priority ranking in search results**
- 🏷️ **"Featured" badge on listing**
- 📧 **Email alerts to matched buyers**
- 📊 **Enhanced analytics** (buyer demographics, engagement metrics)
- 🎨 **Custom listing banner image**

**Best for:** Sellers who want faster results and more visibility

#### Premium Listing
**$999/month**

Everything in Featured, plus:
- 👤 **Anonymous listing option** (hide seller identity)
- 📄 **Custom NDA template upload**
- 🔒 **Private listing with access control**
- 📈 **Advanced analytics dashboard**
- 🎯 **Targeted buyer outreach** (platform sends to qualified buyers)
- 💬 **Priority support** (dedicated account manager)

**Best for:** High-value MSPs ($5M+) or confidential sales

### Featured Listing Limits

- Maximum 10 featured listings at any time
- First-come, first-served basis
- Minimum 3-month commitment
- Cancel anytime after minimum period

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Months 1-6 (Liquidity Building)
**Focus:** Success fees only

**Goals:**
- Reach 50+ active listings
- Complete 3-5 transactions
- Prove platform value proposition
- Build buyer and seller trust

**Pricing:**
- Success fees: 3% / 2% / 1% (as defined above)
- All listings are free (no premium tiers yet)
- No buyer subscriptions

**Why:** Need critical mass before charging for visibility. Focus on proving the platform facilitates real deals.

### Phase 2: Months 7-12 (Monetization Expansion)
**Focus:** Add Featured Listings

**Goals:**
- Generate $5K-10K/month in listing revenue
- Increase average time-to-deal by 30%
- Test willingness to pay for visibility

**Pricing:**
- Success fees continue
- Introduce Featured ($499/mo) and Premium ($999/mo) listings
- Limit to 10 featured slots to maintain exclusivity
- Still no buyer subscriptions

**Why:** Once you have 50+ listings, competition for visibility increases. Sellers will pay to stand out.

### Phase 3: Month 12+ (Full Monetization)
**Focus:** Add Buyer Subscriptions

**Goals:**
- Generate $20K-50K/month in subscription revenue
- Increase buyer engagement and deal velocity
- Build recurring revenue base

**Pricing:**
- All three revenue streams active
- Success fees + Premium listings + Buyer subscriptions
- Consider volume discounts for consolidators (5+ deals/year)

**Why:** Proven platform with deal flow. Buyers will pay for better access and efficiency.

## Alternative Models Considered

### Model A: Listing Fees Only
**Structure:** $299-$999 per listing for 6 months

**Pros:**
- Immediate revenue
- Predictable cash flow
- Simple to implement

**Cons:**
- ❌ Reduces seller participation
- ❌ No incentive alignment
- ❌ Sellers pay even if deal doesn't close
- ❌ Limits platform growth

**Verdict:** ❌ Not recommended. Creates friction and misaligns incentives.

### Model B: Subscription Only
**Structure:** $99-$499/month for sellers, $199-$999/month for buyers

**Pros:**
- Recurring revenue
- Predictable cash flow
- Easy to forecast

**Cons:**
- ❌ High barrier to entry
- ❌ Sellers may not see ROI
- ❌ Requires large user base to work
- ❌ Doesn't scale with deal value

**Verdict:** ❌ Not recommended as primary model. Better as add-on.

### Model C: Pure Success Fee (No Premium Features)
**Structure:** 3-5% success fee only

**Pros:**
- Perfect incentive alignment
- No friction for sellers
- Scales with platform success

**Cons:**
- ❌ No revenue until deals close
- ❌ Long cash conversion cycle (90-120 days)
- ❌ Leaves money on the table (sellers willing to pay for visibility)

**Verdict:** ⚠️ Good for launch, but add premium features by Month 7.

## Implementation Notes

### Technical Requirements

1. **Success Fee Tracking**
   - Add `successFeePercentage` field to deals table
   - Calculate fee based on deal value and tier
   - Track payment status (pending, paid, refunded)
   - Generate invoices automatically

2. **Premium Listing Management**
   - Add `listingTier` field to listings table (free, featured, premium)
   - Add `featuredUntil` timestamp for expiration
   - Implement featured listing limit (max 10)
   - Build upgrade/downgrade workflow

3. **Payment Processing**
   - Integrate Stripe for subscription billing
   - Integrate Escrow.com for success fees
   - Handle failed payments and retries
   - Generate receipts and invoices

4. **Analytics Dashboard**
   - Track revenue by source (success fees, listings, subscriptions)
   - Monitor conversion rates (listing → deal)
   - Calculate customer lifetime value (LTV)
   - Track churn rate for subscriptions

### Legal Considerations

- Success fees must be disclosed in Terms of Service
- Payment terms must be clear before listing
- Refund policy for failed transactions
- Tax implications (1099 reporting for US sellers)
- Compliance with state business broker laws (if applicable)

## FAQ

**Q: Who pays the success fee - buyer or seller?**  
A: Either party can pay, or they can split it. This is negotiated between buyer and seller. The platform is neutral.

**Q: What if a deal is found on the platform but closed off-platform?**  
A: Our Terms of Service include a "platform facilitation clause" - if parties connect through mspsmarket.com, the success fee applies even if closed elsewhere. Enforced through contracts signed during NDA phase.

**Q: Can sellers list on multiple platforms?**  
A: Yes, but we encourage exclusivity by offering a 20% discount on success fees for exclusive listings.

**Q: What if a deal falls through after closing?**  
A: Success fee is non-refundable once the transaction closes. However, if there's fraud or misrepresentation, we'll investigate and may issue refunds.

**Q: How do you verify deal completion?**  
A: Both parties must confirm completion on the platform. We also integrate with Escrow.com to track fund transfers.
