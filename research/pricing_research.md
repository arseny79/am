# MSP M&A Marketplace Pricing Research

## Traditional M&A Broker/Advisor Fees

### Success-Based Commission Structure

**Small Deals (Under $10M)** - Most relevant for MSP businesses
- Commission range: **4% to 12%**
- Industry standard for businesses under $1M: **8% to 12%**
- Businesses $1M to $5M: **Lower percentage** (typically 6-10%)
- Most common rate: **10%**

**Mid-Market Deals ($10M-$50M)**
- Commission range: **2% to 5%**
- Often includes minimum fees of $250K+

**Large Deals ($50M+)**
- Commission range: **1% to 3%**
- Flat fees common instead of percentages

### Additional Fees

**Retainer/Upfront Fees:**
- Deals under $1M: $45K-$55K retainer
- Deals $1M-$10M: $56K-$80K retainer
- Monthly retainers: $500-$5,000 for 7-figure deals

**Valuation Fees:**
- $7,000 to $20,000 for business valuation services

### Lehman Formula (Traditional Tiered Structure)

A common tiered commission structure:
- 5% on the first $1 million
- 4% on the second $1 million
- 3% on the third $1 million
- 2% on the fourth $1 million
- 1% on amounts over $4 million

## Online Marketplace Pricing Models

### Flippa (Online Business Marketplace)

**Listing Fees:**
- Standard: $199 (6-month term, one-time upfront)
- Premium: $799 (6-month term, one-time upfront)
- M&A Broker: $899 (6-month term, one-time upfront)

**Success Fees:**
- 9% success fee (paid after sale)
- Significantly lower than traditional brokers (which charge 8-12%)

**Key Features:**
- No monthly billing
- Account manager support
- AI buyer matching
- LOI and APA builder tools

### BizBuySell (Business-for-Sale Marketplace)

**Subscription Model - No Success Fees:**
- Basic: $66-$90/month (depending on term length)
- Showcase: $90-$110/month
- Diamond: $200-$260/month
- Minimum 3-month commitment
- NO commission or success fee on sale

**Pricing Breakdown (6-month term):**
- Basic: $395.70 total ($65.95/month)
- Showcase: $539.70 total ($89.95/month)
- Diamond: $1,199.70 total ($199.95/month)

### Investors Club

**Free Model:**
- No listing fees
- No success fees/commissions
- Sellers keep 100% of proceeds
- Revenue from buyer memberships and upgrades

## Competitive Pricing Analysis

### Market Positioning

The MSP M&A marketplace sits between two extremes:

1. **High-Touch Traditional Brokers** - 8-12% commission, full-service support
2. **Low-Cost/Free Platforms** - Subscription fees or free, self-service

### Target Market: Small to Mid-Size MSP Sales

Most MSP businesses fall in the **$100K to $5M** range, where:
- Traditional brokers charge **8-12%** ($8K-$600K in fees)
- Online marketplaces charge **0-9%** + listing fees
- Sellers are price-sensitive but need some support

### Competitive Advantages to Leverage

1. **Niche Focus** - MSP-specific (vs general business marketplaces)
2. **Built-in Tools** - Valuation calculator, NDA management, messaging
3. **Lower Overhead** - Automated platform vs human brokers
4. **Transparency** - Clear pricing, no hidden fees

- Filters out non-serious sellers (listing fee)
- Aligns incentives with successful sales (success fee)
- Generates revenue even from unsuccessful listings

- More sustainable than free models

## Implementation Recommendations

### Phase 1: Launch Pricing (First 6 Months)
- Offer **50% off listing fees** to early adopters
- Keep success fees as stated
- Goal: Build marketplace liquidity (get listings)

### Phase 2: Standard Pricing (After 6 Months)
- Full pricing as outlined above
- Monitor conversion rates and adjust

### Phase 3: Optimization (12+ Months)
- A/B test pricing tiers
- Consider volume discounts for brokers/aggregators
- Evaluate buyer subscription uptake

### Metrics to Track
- Listing conversion rate (visitors → listings)
- Sale completion rate (listings → sales)
- Average time to sale
- Customer acquisition cost
- Lifetime value per seller
