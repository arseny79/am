# AM public-site response plan vs IGABroker

## Goal
Make **AM / acquisitions.market** look more credible, more specialized, and more useful to a cold visitor than IGABroker, without giving up the broader marketplace-engine vision.

## Core strategic move
Do **not** make AM look like a generic marketplace.

Make it look like:
- a serious acquisition platform
- with a dedicated **iGaming acquisition surface**
- backed by real workflow infrastructure
- and able to serve broader digital assets after trust is won

## Positioning line
**Acquisitions.market helps buyers, sellers, and brokers transact digital asset and online business deals with structured access, qualification, and deal workflow.**

For the public iGaming surface, use a tighter angle:

**Buy, sell, and broker iGaming and digital asset deals with buyer qualification, NDA-gated access, and structured deal rooms.**

## What AM must beat IGABroker on publicly
1. Better product credibility
2. Better process clarity
3. Better trust signals
4. Better niche landing pages
5. Better explanation of why the platform is safer and more structured than a broker-only site
