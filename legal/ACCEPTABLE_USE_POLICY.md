# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last Updated:** April 2026

**Website:** acquisitions.market

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines prohibited uses of the acquisitions.market Platform. By using the Platform, you agree to comply with this policy.

Violations of this AUP may result in suspension or termination of your account, removal of content, and potential legal action.

## 2. Prohibited Activities

### 2.1 Illegal Activities

You may not use the Platform to:

- Violate any local, state, national, or international law or regulation
- Engage in or promote illegal activities
- Facilitate money laundering or terrorist financing
- Violate export control or sanctions laws
- Engage in securities fraud or market manipulation
- Violate anti-corruption or bribery laws

### 2.2 Fraudulent Conduct

You may not:

- Post false, misleading, or fraudulent business listings
- Misrepresent your identity, credentials, or authority
- Provide false financial information or fabricated documents
- Impersonate another person or entity
- Create fake accounts or use bots to manipulate the Platform
- Engage in any form of fraud or deception

### 2.3 Intellectual Property Infringement

You may not:

- Infringe on copyrights, trademarks, patents, or other intellectual property rights
- Post content you do not have the right to share
- Use another company's trademarks or branding without permission
- Plagiarize content from other sources
- Circumvent digital rights management (DRM) or copy protection

### 2.4 Harmful Content

You may not post or transmit:

- Malware, viruses, trojans, or other malicious code
- Content that is defamatory, libelous, or slanderous
- Hate speech or content that promotes discrimination
- Violent, graphic, or disturbing content
- Sexually explicit or pornographic content
- Content that harasses, threatens, or bullies others

### 2.5 Privacy Violations

You may not:

- Collect personal information about other users without consent
- Share confidential information in violation of NDAs
- Dox or publicly expose private information about individuals
- Access another user's account without authorization
- Share login credentials with third parties

### 2.6 Platform Abuse

You may not:

- Use automated systems (bots, scrapers, crawlers) to access the Platform
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to Platform systems or data
- Interfere with or disrupt the Platform's operation
- Overload servers with excessive requests (DDoS attacks)
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble Platform code
- Remove or alter copyright notices or security features
- Create derivative works based on the Platform

### 2.7 Circumvention

You may not:

- Attempt to bypass Platform fees by conducting transactions off-platform
- Encourage other users to transact outside the Platform to avoid fees
- Use the Platform solely to collect leads for external services
- Manipulate or game Platform features (e.g., fake reviews, inflated metrics)

### 2.8 Spam and Unsolicited Communications

You may not:

- Send spam or unsolicited commercial messages to other users
- Post repetitive or irrelevant content
- Engage in excessive self-promotion unrelated to legitimate listings
- Use the Platform for email harvesting or lead generation for unrelated businesses

### 2.9 Unfair or Deceptive Practices

You may not:

- Bid on or express interest in listings with no genuine intent to transact
- Waste sellers' time with frivolous inquiries
- Pressure or coerce other users into transactions
- Engage in bid rigging or price manipulation
- Create artificial scarcity or urgency through deceptive tactics

## 3. Listing Standards

### 3.1 Seller Obligations

Sellers must ensure that listings:

- Accurately represent the business or asset being sold
- Include truthful financial information
- Disclose all material facts that could affect value
- Do not exaggerate or misrepresent business or asset performance
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

### 3.2 Prohibited Listings

You may not list businesses or assets that:

- Are involved in illegal activities
- Violate intellectual property rights
- Are subject to unresolved legal disputes preventing sale
- Do not actually exist or are not owned by the seller
- Are listed solely to collect buyer information without intent to sell

## 4. Communication Standards

### 4.1 Professional Conduct

All communications on the Platform must be:

- Professional and respectful
- Relevant to the transaction or inquiry
- Free from harassment, threats, or abuse
- Truthful and not misleading

### 4.2 Prohibited Communications

You may not:

- Harass, threaten, or intimidate other users
- Use offensive, abusive, or discriminatory language
- Share confidential information publicly
- Solicit users for unrelated products or services
- Engage in phishing or social engineering attacks

## 5. Data Usage Restrictions

### 5.1 Permitted Uses

You may use data from the Platform only for:

- Evaluating businesses or assets you are genuinely interested in acquiring
- Managing your own listings and transactions
- Communicating with users you have legitimate business relationships with

### 5.2 Prohibited Uses

You may not:

- Scrape or systematically download Platform data
- Use Platform data for competitive intelligence unrelated to transactions
- Build databases or mailing lists from Platform data
- Share or sell Platform data to third parties
- Use Platform data for purposes other than intended Platform use

## 6. Security Responsibilities

### 6.1 Account Security

You must:

- Keep your login credentials confidential
- Use a strong, unique password
- Enable two-factor authentication if available
- Notify us immediately of any unauthorized access
- Log out from shared or public devices

### 6.2 Prohibited Security Actions

You may not:

- Share your account with others
- Attempt to hack or breach Platform security
- Exploit security vulnerabilities
- Access areas of the Platform you are not authorized to access
- Interfere with security features or authentication mechanisms

## 7. Reporting Violations

### 7.1 How to Report

If you encounter content or behavior that violates this AUP, please report it immediately to:

**Email:** abuse@acquisitions.market

Include:

- Description of the violation
- URL or location of the violating content
- Screenshots or evidence (if applicable)
- Your contact information

### 7.2 Our Response

Upon receiving a report, we will:

- Investigate the reported violation
- Take appropriate action (warning, content removal, account suspension/termination)
- Notify you of the outcome (when appropriate)
- Cooperate with law enforcement if illegal activity is involved

## 8. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this AUP may result in:

### 8.1 Warnings

First-time or minor violations may result in a warning and request to correct the behavior.

### 8.2 Content Removal

We may remove content that violates this AUP without prior notice.

### 8.3 Account Suspension

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary suspension of your account.

### 8.4 Account Termination

Severe violations or repeated offenses may result in permanent termination of your account and ban from the Platform.

### 8.5 Legal Action

We reserve the right to pursue legal action for violations that:

- Cause harm to acquisitions.market or its users
- Involve illegal activity
- Result in financial loss or damages
- Violate intellectual property rights

### 8.6 Law Enforcement Cooperation

We will cooperate with law enforcement investigations and may disclose user information when required by law or to prevent harm.

## 9. No Liability for User Conduct

acquisitions.market is not responsible for:

- User-generated content or conduct
- Violations of this AUP by users
- Damages resulting from prohibited activities
- Disputes between users

Users are solely responsible for their own conduct and compliance with this AUP.

## 10. Modifications to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this AUP at any time. Changes will be effective upon posting to the Platform.

Material changes will be communicated via:

- Email notification to registered users
- Prominent notice on the Platform

Your continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the modified AUP.
